# The Right to Information Act, 2005

The text of The Right to Information Act, 2005, with one commit per section of each amending act. Commit trailers give the
amending act, its section, the Gazette reference, the date it was enacted and the date it came
into force. Commits follow the order of enactment, and tags such as `1977-12-31` mark the law as
published at the end of each year.

**Unofficial, and not legal advice.** The official text is at https://rti.dopt.gov.in/Writereaddata/RTI%20Act,%202005%20(Amended)-English%20Version.PDF
